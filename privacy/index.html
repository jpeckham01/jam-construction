Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

JAM Construction Services LLC ("JAM Construction", "we", "us") is a home improvement contractor serving Baltimore County, Maryland. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit jamconstructionmd.com, call or text us, or hire us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you call or text us for an estimate or about a project, we receive your phone number and whatever you choose to share, such as your name, the project address, a description of the work and photos.
  • Project information. If you hire us, we keep the details needed to do the job, such as your address, the scope of work, estimates, invoices and payment records.
  • Website information. This website has no forms, accounts or advertising trackers and does not use cookies. Like any website, the companies that host and deliver it (GitHub Pages for hosting and Google Fonts for typefaces) automatically receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to send you the page.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions, prepare estimates and schedule and complete your project.
  • To send you texts or calls about your estimate or project, if you contacted us first.
  • To keep business, tax and legal records.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Photos of our work

We may post photos of finished or in-progress projects in the "Recent jobs" section of this website. We only show the work itself: we do not publish customer names, house numbers or street names, and we remove location data from photos before posting. If you would like a photo of your property taken down, call or text us and we will remove it.

Text messages (SMS)

If you text us or agree to receive texts from us, we use your phone number only to communicate with you about your estimate or project. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving messages and HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. We use service providers (such as our phone and text messaging provider) only to deliver messages on our behalf.

When we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business (for example our phone and text messaging provider, website host and accountant), with suppliers or subcontractors when needed to complete your project, or when required by law. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep project and payment records as long as needed for warranty, tax and legal purposes, and other messages only as long as they are useful for your project.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct or delete it (unless we must keep it for legal reasons), or ask us to stop contacting you, by calling or texting us at (443) 901-8869.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
